# Riggverk 2026.10.7

## New

- A new look. The terminal-style screens are gone: a sidebar lists Overview, your agents, History, Library and Settings (keys 1 to 8 always open the same place), and a top bar has Check and Update all. Everything is mouse-first, with the keyboard shortcuts as accelerators (`?` lists them). Neutral graphite with a matching light theme, the system font for text, and motion that follows Settings > Motion (#94, #102, #103, #105, #108, #109)
- Overview answers "is my setup OK?" at the top: "Your setup works." or "Everything is set up.", with one line that counts only what is not zero. Each agent has a card, and the last update shows what changed and what was left for you, with Undo (#102)
- Decisions are shown where they belong. A file you edited gets a card that says why Riggverk left it alone; Compare opens the side-by-side difference right there, and the two choices say what happens next. Nothing is written before you preview it, Enter never applies, and `y` or the Apply button does (#105, #107)
- Codex settings: the table shows Riggverk's value next to yours for each key Riggverk manages. Keep mine keeps your value; Use Riggverk defaults sets only the keys that differ, after a preview, with a backup and Undo. Riggverk refuses instead of touching anything the preview didn't show (#99, #107)
- History lists every update, also after a restart, with what changed, what was already current and what was left for you. Undo is offered on the newest one and always asks first (#98, #106)
- Skill packs: put your own skills in `~/.config/riggverk/packs/<name>/skills/` and Riggverk installs them like its own, with backups and Undo. If a pack goes missing or can't be read, its skills stay installed until you choose Forget pack in the Library. Packs that others can write to are refused (#101, #104)

## Install

Unzip `Riggverk-v2026.10.7-macOS.zip`, move `Riggverk.app` to `/Applications` and open it. Riggverk is ad hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS stops the first launch: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway next to the message about Riggverk.
