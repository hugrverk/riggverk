# Riggverk 2026.10.11

## Changes

- The flow map on the Overview shows each agent with its own icon, and its state as a small badge on the icon: a ring when it is fine, a filled square when it needs your decision, a dashed ring when it is off (#126)
- The sidebar lists only the agents you use; the ones that are off show in the Overview's flow map, where a click takes you to Settings (#126)
- The progress and outcome of an update now sit in a bar of their own at the bottom and never cover the page above them. Sheets and dialogs in the Library have more room to breathe (#126)

## Install

Unzip `Riggverk-v2026.10.11-macOS.zip`, move `Riggverk.app` to `/Applications` and open it. Riggverk is ad hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS stops the first launch: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway next to the message about Riggverk.
