# Riggverk 2026.10.15

## New

- The Overview's flow map stays short however many agents Riggverk knows: the agents that are off share one node at the bottom ("6 off", with their icons), and selecting it lists them with a way to Settings. Agents that are on keep their own rows and curves (#138)

## Install

Unzip `Riggverk-v2026.10.15-macOS.zip`, move `Riggverk.app` to `/Applications` and open it. Riggverk is ad hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS stops the first launch: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway next to the message about Riggverk.
