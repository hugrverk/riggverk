# Riggverk 2026.10.3

## Changes

- Riggverk no longer keeps a copy of its bundle in your workspace folder (`~/.config/agent-workspace`). The Library and health read the bundle inside the app, and the folder now holds only Riggverk's state (`.agent-workspace/`), your personal docs (`docs/context/`) and anything you put there yourself (#76)
- Old copies from earlier versions show on the `workspace` row as "N old copies to remove". Press `u` to review: every removal is listed per folder and backed up first, and `z` restores them. Nothing is removed at launch (#76, #78)
- Files you edited, and files Riggverk cannot prove it wrote, are left in place and shown as "left in place". To keep a copy of everything, cancel the review, or press `z` after applying (#76)
- The workspace view no longer shows the data folder's git branch or uncommitted changes; GIT_VERSIONING.md now covers versioning `docs/context/` only (#77)

## Fixes

- Files that only early test builds shipped are also cleaned up when they are unchanged (#78)

## Install

Unzip `Riggverk-v2026.10.3-macOS.zip`, move `Riggverk.app` to `/Applications` and open it. Riggverk is ad hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS stops the first launch: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway next to the message about Riggverk.
