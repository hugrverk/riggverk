# Riggverk 2026.11.7

## New

- Riggverk now comes as a disk image: open it and drag Riggverk to Applications. One universal app runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, and `SHA256SUMS` lists the checksums of the downloads (#187)
- The groundwork for updating Riggverk from inside the app. It stays off until a later release turns it on, and then asks you once before it checks (#188)

## Install

Open `Riggverk-v2026.11.7-macOS.dmg`, drag Riggverk to Applications and open it from there (or unzip `Riggverk-v2026.11.7-macOS.zip` and move `Riggverk.app` to `/Applications`). One app runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Riggverk is ad hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS stops the first launch: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway next to the message about Riggverk. `SHA256SUMS` lists the checksums of both downloads (`shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing`).
