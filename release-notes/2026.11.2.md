## New

- Use a silver accent that matches the rune mark (#167)

## Install

Unzip `Riggverk-v2026.11.2-macOS.zip`, move `Riggverk.app` to `/Applications` and open it. Riggverk is ad hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS stops the first launch: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway next to the message about Riggverk.
