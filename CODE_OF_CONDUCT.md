# Code of conduct

Riggverk follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers at hej@hugrverk.se. Do not use public issues for it. Reports are read by the maintainers only and handled confidentially.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide on and apply the consequences described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, from a private warning to a permanent ban.
